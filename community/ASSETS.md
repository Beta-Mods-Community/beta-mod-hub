# Source and asset scope

The software license applies to original project source, not to user uploads,
private databases, account information or third-party mods. Dependencies retain
their own licenses. Beta Mods is independent of Nexus Mods and is not endorsed
by Nexus Mods or a game publisher.

The public contributor edition uses original geometric SVG placeholders for
the homepage illustration and icon. The live site's dragon illustration and
favicon are not included pending a documented redistribution rights review.
The placeholders are included under the same license as the project source.
This substitution does not change the running website.

Lucide icons are provided by the lucide-react dependency under its upstream
license. Fonts loaded through next/font retain their upstream licenses.
Contributor submissions must include permission and attribution for any new
artwork, fonts, screenshots or copied code. Do not submit other authors' mod
archives as test fixtures without their permission.
